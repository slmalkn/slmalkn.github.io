Zigs & Drips: Split the Water – Privacy Policy

Developer: MileStone Apps · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game “Zigs & Drips: Split the Water” (the “Game”) handles and why. The short version: the Game has no accounts, we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and your game progress stays on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Level progress, stars, coins, settings and your chosen language are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Information handled by third-party services

The Game may use the services below. They may collect information automatically, as described in their own policies.

ServicePurposeData it may collect
Google AdMobShowing ads, including optional rewarded adsAdvertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions
Google Play BillingOptional in-app purchasesPurchase records. Payments are processed by Google; we never see your card details.

Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Some versions of the Game do not include all of these services. A version without ads or analytics does not collect the related data.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads or analytics are used. You can change your choice later from the Game’s settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).
  • Buying “Remove Ads” removes the ads shown between levels. Rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch them.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it. Data collected by the services in section 2 is kept according to their retention rules; analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no account for you, most requests are handled through your device settings and the consent options in the Game. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
